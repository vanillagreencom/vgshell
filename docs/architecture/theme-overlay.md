# Theme overlay

Covers: shell/plugins/vgs.themes/Browser.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/BrowserLogic.js, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/Service.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/ThemeView.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/ThemePreviews.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/ThemeCard.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/WallpaperView.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/WallpaperCard.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/DesktopPreview.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/ThemePaletteStrip.qml, shell/plugins/vgs.themes/Files.js, scripts/smoke/rows/theme-browser.sh, scripts/test-themes-browser.js, scripts/qml-tests/tst_themes_browser.qml

The `vgs.themes` overlay: one full-screen browser with a theme view and a wallpaper view, and the service whose shortcuts summon it. Both views drive the `theme` capability of [theme-capability.md](theme-capability.md); the rest of the plugin is [theme-capability.md § Plugin](theme-capability.md). `BrowserLogic.js` holds every decision the views make, with no QML object and no I/O.

## Overlay

- **Service.** The service registers one shortcut per view of `BrowserLogic.VIEWS`: `vgs.themes:themes`, which the manifest binds to `SUPER+T`, and `vgs.themes:wallpapers`, bound to `SUPER+W`. Pressing one summons the plugin's overlay with `{ "view": <name> }`.
- **Browser.** The overlay entry is one browser with a view table, `themes` and `wallpapers`; a payload without `view` opens `themes`. A second summon of the shown view closes it, and a summon of the other view switches to it. Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab, Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp switch the top-level tabs. Learned user focus binds call the view's `navigate(direction)` and step the carousel. The view lists shipped, installed and catalog packages in one card list. All and Installed narrow the list, and Alt+I toggles that control. Type-to-filter matches the package name or the title label. A card image is the package's first background, else the catalog thumbnail, else a palette card from the package or catalog palette. Every card with a palette draws its palette strip, even when an image is ready. Badges name the displayed package, catalog packages that are not installed and pinned wallpaper archives.
- **Browser apply.** Enter or a click on the selected card installs a catalog package when needed, applies it, waits until `Theme.name` names the package, then refreshes catalog and image state. If the applied catalog package has a positive wallpaper size and its imagery is not installed, the view shows a `Dialog` asking to download its wallpapers. Download runs `wallpapers(name)`, reads progress from `last.downloading`, then applies the package again. Not now leaves the package applied. A failure line names install, apply or download; a partial apply names the Themes panel as the place with target detail.
- **Wallpaper browser.** The `wallpapers` view reads the service's retained image list and catalog on open and splits them in `BrowserLogic.wallpaperCards`. Theme lists the applied package's images, then a download card when its catalog entry is installed, pins an archive of more than 0 bytes and has no imagery installed, or an update card when the index pins another archive than the one unpacked (`wallpaperOffer`). All lists every image, packages in name order, then the user folder, each badged with its package's label or `User folder`. With two screens or more a scope control shows, All monitors first on every open; This monitor passes `shell.screens.current.name` to `set`, All monitors passes `*`. Enter or Space on an image sets it and holds the browser open until the set answers: it closes on success and shows a failure line otherwise. Enter or Space on the card runs `wallpapers` on the lane, with `{ update: true }` for the update card, shows progress from `last.downloading`, applies the package again so its image shows, reads the lists again and stays open. Each view loads images under the changed package's stamp. `BrowserLogic.railKey` folds the stamp into card identity. `Files.stampedUrl` keeps an unchanged image URL across opens, so Qt reuses its decoded pixmap. A watched image or package change advances that package's stamp, so a replaced file loads again. The selection starts on the image the scope shows, from `WallpaperState`'s `path` or the output's `screenPaths` entry, and follows it until a key or a click moves it. The keys are `BrowserLogic.WALLPAPER_KEYS`: Left and Up step back, Right and Down step forward, Home, End, Enter, Space and Escape keep their direct meaning, Alt+S flips the source, Alt+M flips the monitor scope while its control shows, and Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab, Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp switch the top-level tabs. The Alt+letter rule keeps every secondary toggle clear of type-to-filter in the theme view and uses the same rule in the wallpaper view. An item of the view holds the keyboard and runs the key table, and the rail does not take Tab. A segment click focuses its control and a click on the chosen segment emits no `activated`, so each segmented control in both views hands the keyboard back whenever it takes it, one event-loop turn later. The browser has no Add to theme, Remove or Delete ([D025](../decisions/D025-no-theme-override-layer.md): a package changes only as a whole installed package; the user's own images live in the user folder).
- **Layout.** Each view composes one `Pane` of the `overlay` class, fitted and centred on the output, as Omarchy centres its picker ([design-quality.md § Containers](design-quality.md#containers)). The header holds the tabs over the view's segmented controls, each `stack.inline` from its key cap. The body is the rail: `CardCarousel`'s `implicitHeight`, or `Pane.bodyRoom`, the room the pane leaves its body on the output. A click on the band beside the cards reaches the scrim and closes the browser, since a `ScrollArea` whose content fits takes no press. The footer, at most `carousel.expandedWidth` wide, the expanded card's width at unit 1, holds the selected card's name in `text.display`, its badges and the filter, then the running step, the failure lines and `KeyHints`, one cap and its action per pair. With no card to show, `EmptyState` says why: loading, nothing installed, or a filter no card matches, whose Clear filter does what Escape does; the wallpaper view's empty Theme source offers Show every source, as Alt+S does. Both hand the keyboard back to the view. The palette card's and the offer card's names keep the lean from each side, and the preview's windows keep `safeInset` from the card's edge and clear the theme's own window corner.
- **Omarchy.** The browser copies Omarchy's full-screen scrim, angled-card geometry, type-to-filter, Escape clear-then-close and settle-before-reveal through `CardCarousel`. It replaces Omarchy's selection-file and done-file round trip with one `theme` capability job and `last`. It has no Starred filter because VGS has no starred theme state. Omarchy's background switcher (`bin/omarchy-theme-bg-switcher` at `basecamp/omarchy` `e332dc9`) lists the applied theme's images and the user folder for that theme with the current image selected, and sets one background for every monitor. The wallpaper view takes the preselected current image. It adds the All source, the scope control and the download or update card, because VGS has per-screen images ([D039](../decisions/D039-per-screen-wallpaper-map.md)) and catalog archives the user downloads on demand. VGS differs from Omarchy's Tab-to-step model because the browser has two top-level tabs and secondary toggles; Tab switches tabs, and Alt+letter keys activate the toggles shown on their controls.

## Invariants

1. The `vgs.themes` service registers `vgs.themes:themes`, the Hyprland layer binds it to `SUPER+T`, the overlay opens with keyboard capture, learned focus binds step its carousel, it focuses, filters, pages, installs and applies a catalog card, offers the wallpaper download, handles Download and Not now, shows download progress from `last.downloading`, refuses unknown browser payloads and closes on Escape, the shortcut and the scrim, a click on the band beside the cards included, only when no browser job runs. The empty state's Clear filter clears the filter and hands the keyboard back to the rail, and the wallpaper view's Show every source switches to All and hands the keys back to the view. Every shown non-refused theme card carries a visible palette strip with at least one colour. The chrome lies inside the output less `inset.overlay`, the header above the rail, the rail holding the selected card above the caption and centred, and the key line on one line. Enforced by `scripts/smoke/rows/theme-browser.sh`, which drives the nested seat and reads `ThemeView`, `ThemeCard`, `Badge`, `Dialog`, card images and palette strips through the smoke probe. Its controls remove the Alt+I action and hide the palette strip, move one box per layout rule in a copy of the reading, and take copies of `ThemeView.qml` and `WallpaperView.qml` that drop each recovery handler, its focus restoration, or plant a press-taking area on the rail's band.
2. The `vgs.themes` service registers `vgs.themes:wallpapers`, the Hyprland layer binds it to `SUPER+W`, and the overlay opens the wallpaper view holding the keyboard, lists the applied package's images under Theme and every source's under All, flips the source on Alt+S and the scope on Alt+M while two screens show the scope control, switches the top tabs on Tab, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab, Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp, starts each open on All monitors, selects the image the scope shows, sets an image on the browser's screen alone under This monitor and on every screen with each screen's own cleared under All monitors, closes once the set lands, runs the update card on the download lane with `--update`, applies the package again and stays open with the new image listed and an image replaced under its name drawn anew, reads that image anew on the next open, and after a click on the chosen segment steps the rail on Right. In the theme view the same click leaves the scope on Right. Enforced by `scripts/smoke/rows/theme-browser.sh`, which drives the nested seat with a second headless output and reads `WallpaperView` through the probe and each screen's drawn image through the background, with copies of `WallpaperView.qml` that set every image as the current one, keep each card's identity across an update and keep the keyboard on the source control, and a copy of `ThemeView.qml` that keeps it on the scope control, as its controls. The view's decisions, `BrowserLogic.js`'s sources, scopes, cards, card rule, keys, rail identity, selection and lines, and `Files.js`'s stamped URL, are enforced by `scripts/test-themes-browser.js`, with a copy per rule as its controls.
3. A catalog card's sharpened preview, which the theme view asks `theme.preview` for once the rail rests on the card, is kept by `ThemePreviews.qml`, which the browser owns and not the view. The capability hands its answer to a waiter that lives as long as the plugin instance, the browser, and a preview is no step that holds the browser, so a view switch can destroy the theme view while one runs: the answer lands in the browser, and the next theme view draws it on its rail without asking again. A theme view that goes clears the card it wanted, so an answer that lands with no theme view shown starts no other preview. Enforced by `scripts/qml-tests/tst_themes_browser.qml`, which keeps the stand-in capability's callback, moves the rail to a second card, switches to the wallpaper view and calls it, with copies of `ThemeView.qml`, `ThemePreviews.qml` and `Browser.qml` in `scripts/test-qml-unit.sh` as its controls.

The themes service retains browser data and precomputed theme cards in memory. A card keeps its content key while its content stays equal. The displayed badge follows the current theme without rebuilding the catalog. `BrowserData.qml` reads its latest serialized revision on creation and polls revisions in process. Installed cards come from `shell.theme.listing`. Catalog refresh runs behind the browser. Package image stamps change only when watched content changes. The image cache therefore survives reopen. The selected desktop preview loads asynchronously. The cached image or palette remains visible until the preview wallpaper is ready. `scripts/smoke/rows/theme-latency.sh` reads window frame timestamps after physical keys and enforces the warm open and desktop change bounds.
